Ask the person in this Linear session to choose between a few options. Linear shows the question with each option as a button, and the person's pick, or a free-text reply instead, arrives as their next message.

Use it when the answer is one of a short list you can name, such as which approach to take, whether to open a pull request, or which of several files to change. For an open question, end your turn with the question as your final message instead.

Give 2 to 10 short, distinct options, each a few words. This ends your turn: call it last, then finish with the question as your final message.
